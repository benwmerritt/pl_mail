**Type a time to snooze or send later.** The snooze and send-later menus have a field at the top
that reads *tue 3pm*, *2 days*, *next month*, *tomorrow 9:30* or *in 3 hours*, and the same in
German (*di 15 Uhr*, *2 Tage*, *morgen früh*). It shows the time it understood, and **Enter** takes
it. With a keyboard the field has the focus as the menu opens, so **b** and the time is all it
takes. No schema change.
